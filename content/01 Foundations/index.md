# Foundations

<p class="domain-tagline">Before you can figure out what's true, you need to know how to tell truth from guesswork.</p>
</div>

> [!abstract]
> This is where HumanOS starts. It's not facts about the world — it's the toolkit for telling what's actually true from what just sounds true: math, logic, the scientific method, systems thinking, critical thinking, and the study of knowledge itself. Every other domain in HumanOS leans on these tools to hold up.

# Why This Actually Matters

People are naturally curious — about the universe, about life, about how to live. But being curious doesn't mean you land on the right answer. Throughout history, people have explained the world through tradition, gut feeling, religion, philosophy, and science. Some of those explanations stuck around because they kept matching reality. Others got replaced once better evidence showed up.

That's the gap this domain closes. You need to learn **how good knowledge gets built** before you can trust **what it tells you** — otherwise every mistake in your thinking gets passed down into everything you build on top of it.
