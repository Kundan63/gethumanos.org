# Technology & Engineering

<p class="domain-tagline">How humans transform scientific knowledge into technologies that improve civilization.</p>
</div>

> [!abstract]
> Technology is the application of scientific knowledge, engineering principles, and human creativity to develop tools, machines, systems, and processes that solve practical problems and extend human capabilities. Engineering is the disciplined process of designing, building, testing, and improving these technologies. Every bridge, smartphone, computer, airplane, vaccine, satellite, internet connection, and robot exists because engineering transformed scientific knowledge into practical solutions.

# Why This Actually Matters

Technology influences nearly every aspect of life — health, education, transportation, communication, energy, food production, business, and scientific discovery. Understanding technology enables people to use it wisely, evaluate its impacts, and contribute to future innovation.
