# Human Development

> *Human Development is the lifelong process through which a single fertilized cell grows into a complex human being and continues changing from conception until death. Understanding human development explains not only how humans grow, but also how each stage of life builds the foundation for the next.*

---

# What This Section Covers

From the first cell division to old age, human development moves through predictable stages, each shaped by the interaction of genes, biology, environment, and experience.
