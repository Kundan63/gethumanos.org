# Why This Actually Matters

Every decision you make changes your biology. The food you eat changes your cells. The sleep you get changes your brain. Exercise changes your muscles, heart, hormones, and immune system. Understanding the human organism helps explain why diseases occur, why healthy habits work, why medicines work, and why prevention matters.

This is knowledge you apply every day — not just knowledge you remember.
