# HumanOS Framework

> **The HumanOS Framework explains how reality is connected.**
>
> It provides the conceptual model that links every discipline within HumanOS into one unified understanding of reality, human capability, and civilization.

---

# Purpose

HumanOS is built upon one fundamental observation.

Reality is not fragmented.

Everything depends upon something else.

Understanding reality therefore requires understanding relationships, not isolated facts.

The HumanOS Framework provides those relationships.

# Framework Rules

Every HumanOS note should:

- Explain one idea clearly.
- Connect to previous knowledge.
- Link to future knowledge.
- Show dependencies.
- Show consequences.
- Show practical applications.
- Show human relevance.

If a note cannot be connected, it is incomplete.

---

# The HumanOS Network

HumanOS is not organized as a tree.

It behaves as a network.

Every concept should connect to multiple others.

```text
Physics

├── Chemistry
├── Mathematics
├── Engineering

Chemistry

├── Biology
├── Medicine
├── Materials Science

Biology

├── Neuroscience
├── Psychology
├── Evolution
```

The more connections a concept has, the greater its explanatory power.

---

# Framework Principle

> **Understanding grows through connection.**
>
> HumanOS teaches reality as an interconnected network of systems rather than a collection of isolated disciplines. Every topic is understood by examining what it depends upon, how it works, what it influences, and why it matters. The strength of HumanOS lies not only in the quality of its individual notes, but in the quality of the relationships between them.
