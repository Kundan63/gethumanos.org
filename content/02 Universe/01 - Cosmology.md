# Cosmology

> *Understanding the story of the Universe is the first step toward understanding our own story.*

---

# What is Cosmology?

Cosmology is the scientific study of the **Universe as a whole**.

It seeks to understand:

- How the Universe began.
- How it has evolved.
- What it is made of.
- Why galaxies, stars, and planets formed.
- How the Universe may change in the future.

Unlike [[Astronomy]], which studies individual celestial objects, cosmology studies the entire Universe as one connected system. It combines observations, mathematics, and physics to explain the history and large-scale evolution of the cosmos.

---

# Why does Cosmology exist?

For thousands of years humans have looked at the night sky and wondered where everything came from.

Modern cosmology exists because the Universe contains evidence of its own history.

By studying the light from distant galaxies, the expansion of space, and the oldest radiation in the Universe, scientists can reconstruct events that happened billions of years ago.

Cosmology transforms one of humanity's oldest questions into a scientific investigation.

---

# Why should humans care?

Cosmology is not only about distant galaxies. It explains the history that eventually produced Earth, water, the elements in our bodies, life, and human civilization.

> [!success] Understanding cosmology helps humans
> Understand where we came from.
>
> Appreciate Earth's uniqueness.
>
> Develop scientific curiosity.
>
> Build a long-term perspective.
>
> Support scientific discovery.
>
> Better understand our place within reality.

Without cosmology, humanity would understand very little about its own origins.

# Applying this Knowledge

Understanding cosmology can influence how we live. It reminds us to:

> [!tip]
> Appreciate that every human shares a common cosmic history.
>
> Value scientific evidence when exploring reality.
>
> Protect Earth, the only known planet that supports life.
>
> Support scientific research that benefits future generations.
>
> Think beyond immediate concerns and consider humanity's long-term future.

Understanding the Universe helps place our individual lives within a much larger story.
