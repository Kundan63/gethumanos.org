# Cosmology

> *Understanding the story of the Universe is the first step toward understanding our own story.*

---

# What is Cosmology?

Cosmology is the scientific study of the **Universe as a whole**.

It seeks to understand:

- How the Universe began.
- How it has evolved.
- What it is made of.
- Why galaxies, stars, and planets formed.
- How the Universe may change in the future.

Unlike [[Astronomy]], which studies individual celestial objects, cosmology studies the entire Universe as one connected system. It combines observations, mathematics, and physics to explain the history and large-scale evolution of the cosmos. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# Why does Cosmology exist?

For thousands of years humans have looked at the night sky and wondered where everything came from.

Modern cosmology exists because the Universe contains evidence of its own history.

By studying the light from distant galaxies, the expansion of space, and the oldest radiation in the Universe, scientists can reconstruct events that happened billions of years ago. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Cosmology transforms one of humanity's oldest questions into a scientific investigation.

---

# How does Cosmology work?

Cosmology combines observations with mathematical models and physical laws.

```text
Observe the Universe
          │
          ▼
Collect Evidence
(Galaxies • Light • Radiation)
          │
          ▼
Apply Physics
          │
          ▼
Mathematical Models
          │
          ▼
Test Predictions
          │
          ▼
Improve Understanding
```

As observations improve, cosmological models are refined.

This makes cosmology a continually evolving scientific discipline rather than a collection of permanent answers. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

# Why should humans care?

Cosmology is not only about distant galaxies.

It explains the history that eventually produced:

- Earth
- Water
- The elements in our bodies
- Life
- Human civilization

Understanding cosmology helps humans:

- Understand where we came from.
- Appreciate Earth's uniqueness.
- Develop scientific curiosity.
- Build a long-term perspective.
- Support scientific discovery.
- Better understand our place within reality.

Without cosmology, humanity would understand very little about its own origins. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

# Daily Life Examples

Although cosmology studies the largest scales of reality, its discoveries influence everyday life.

### GPS

Accurate GPS navigation depends on our understanding of space, time, and gravity developed through modern physics and cosmology.

---

### Weather Forecasts

Weather satellites orbit Earth using principles developed through astronomy and space science.

Without them, modern weather prediction would be impossible.

---

### Smartphone Cameras

Many digital imaging technologies used in smartphones were improved through research for astronomical observations.

---

### Medical Imaging

Image-processing techniques originally developed for studying distant galaxies are also used in medical diagnostics.

---

### Artificial Intelligence

Modern cosmology generates enormous amounts of data.

The tools developed to analyze this data have contributed to advances in machine learning, high-performance computing, and artificial intelligence.

# Human Impact

## Human Body

Every human body is built from elements that were created during the evolution of the Universe.

Hydrogen formed shortly after the early Universe.

Carbon, oxygen, calcium, and iron were forged inside stars before becoming part of planets and eventually living organisms.

In a scientific sense,

**humans are part of the Universe becoming aware of itself.** :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
