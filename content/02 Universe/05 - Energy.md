# Energy

> *Energy is what makes change possible. Every heartbeat, thought, sunrise, storm, machine, and civilization exists because energy is constantly flowing and transforming.*

---

# What is Energy?

Energy is the capacity of a physical system to produce change by doing work or transferring heat.

Unlike matter, energy is **not a substance** that can be touched or stored in a container. Instead, it is a measurable property that allows things to move, grow, heat up, shine, react, and transform. Energy exists in many forms and can change from one form to another while the total amount remains conserved in an isolated system.

Common forms of energy include:

- ☀ Radiant (Light)
- 🏃 Kinetic (Motion)
- 🏔 Potential (Stored)
- 🔥 Thermal (Heat)
- 🍎 Chemical
- ⚡ Electrical
- ☢ Nuclear
- 🔊 Sound

Although these forms appear different, they are all different manifestations of the same physical quantity.

---

# Why does Energy exist?

Matter alone cannot create change.

Energy allows matter to move, interact, transform, and perform work.

Because of energy:

- Stars shine.
- Water evaporates.
- Winds blow.
- Plants grow.
- Hearts beat.
- Brains think.
- Civilizations function.

Without energy, the Universe would remain static.

Energy is therefore the driving force behind every natural process and every human activity.

---

Energy is constantly transferred and transformed.

It never disappears—it simply changes form.
