# Universe

<p class="domain-tagline">The origin and framework of everything that exists.</p>
</div>

> [!abstract]
> The Universe is the totality of space, time, matter, energy, and the physical laws that govern them. Everything explored throughout HumanOS ultimately originates from the Universe — stars, planets, oceans, mountains, living organisms, human beings, civilizations, and technology are all products of processes that began billions of years ago. Understanding the Universe provides the context for understanding everything that follows.

# Why This Actually Matters

The Universe is not separate from human life. Every atom in the human body was formed inside ancient stars. Every breath depends on physical laws. Every heartbeat follows chemical and biological processes that originated within the Universe. Every technology humans create depends on the same physical principles that govern galaxies.

Understanding the Universe helps humans understand where we came from, what we are made of, and how we fit into reality.

# What Universe Gives Every Other Domain

| Domain | What Universe Gives It |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Natural Sciences | The physical matter, energy, and laws that chemistry and physics study |
| Life Sciences | The elements and conditions that made biology possible |
| Human | The atoms and physical processes the human body is built from |
| Mind | A physical substrate — matter and energy — that consciousness ultimately runs on |
| Society & Civilization | The shared physical reality every society operates within |
| Technology & Engineering | The physical laws that every invention has to work with |
| Philosophy & Spirituality | The raw questions of origin, existence, and meaning |
| Living Well | A sense of scale and place — where we came from, and what we're made of |

Universe is the root layer of the HumanOS knowledge tree — everything else is built on the matter, energy, space, and time it explains.
