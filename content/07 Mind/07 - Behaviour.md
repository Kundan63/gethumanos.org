# Behaviour

> *Behaviour is the observable actions and responses of an organism to internal and external stimuli. It includes everything people do—from simple reflexes and habits to complex social interactions and goal-directed actions. Human behaviour emerges from the interaction of biology, cognition, emotions, learning, personality, environment, and culture. Psychology studies behaviour scientifically to understand why people act the way they do and how behaviour can change.* :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# What is Behaviour?

Behaviour is **everything an organism does** in response to its internal state and external environment.

Examples include:

- Walking
- Speaking
- Smiling
- Studying
- Eating
- Helping others
- Avoiding danger
- Solving problems
- Communicating

Behaviour is the **observable outcome** of mental and biological processes. While thoughts and emotions occur internally, behaviour is how they become visible to others. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Why does Behaviour exist?

Living organisms must constantly interact with their environment.

They must:

- Find food
- Avoid danger
- Reproduce
- Communicate
- Learn
- Cooperate
- Compete
- Adapt

Behaviour evolved because it allows organisms to respond to changing conditions, increasing survival and reproductive success.

---

# The Big Question

> **Why do humans behave the way they do?**

Behaviour results from the continuous interaction of:

- Biology
- Cognition
- Emotions
- Learning
- Memory
- Personality
- Social environment
- Culture

No single factor fully explains human behaviour.

## 🧬 Biological Factors

Behaviour is influenced by:

- Brain function
- Genetics
- Hormones
- Neurotransmitters
- Physical health

Biology provides the foundation upon which behaviour develops.

---

## 🧠 Cognitive Factors

People interpret situations before acting.

Important cognitive processes include:

- Attention
- Perception
- Memory
- Reasoning
- Expectations

The same event may produce different behaviours because different people think about it differently.

---

## ❤️ Emotional Factors

Emotions strongly influence behaviour.

Examples:

- Fear → Escape
- Anger → Confrontation
- Joy → Celebration
- Sadness → Withdrawal

Healthy behaviour depends partly on emotional regulation.

---

## 📚 Learning

Experience changes behaviour through:

- Classical conditioning
- Operant conditioning
- Observational learning
- Cognitive learning

Learning allows behaviour to adapt throughout life. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

## 👤 Personality

Personality influences consistent patterns of behaviour.

Examples:

- Introversion
- Extraversion
- Conscientiousness
- Agreeableness

Different personalities often respond differently to similar situations.

---

## 🌍 Environment & Culture

Behaviour is shaped by:

- Family
- Education
- Friends
- Society
- Culture
- Economic conditions

Social norms influence which behaviours are encouraged or discouraged.

# Types of Behaviour

```text
Behaviour
      │
 ├── Innate
 ├── Learned
 ├── Voluntary
 ├── Involuntary
 ├── Individual

```

---

## Innate Behaviour

Present at birth.

Examples:

- Reflexes
- Blinking
- Sucking reflex in infants

---

## Learned Behaviour

Develops through experience.

Examples:

- Reading
- Driving
- Speaking a language
- Playing an instrument

---

## Voluntary Behaviour

Intentional actions under conscious control.

Examples:

- Writing
- Exercising
- Studying

---

## Involuntary Behaviour

Automatic actions.

Examples:

- Sneezing
- Pupil dilation
- Reflex responses

---

## Social Behaviour

Interactions with other people.

Examples:

- Cooperation
- Competition
- Communication
- Leadership
- Helping

# Misconceptions

### ❌ Behaviour is controlled only by willpower.

Reality:

Behaviour results from the interaction of biology, learning, emotions, cognition, environment, and social influences.

---

### ❌ People always behave logically.

Reality:

Behaviour is often influenced by emotions, habits, biases, and environmental cues.

---

### ❌ Behaviour cannot change.

Reality:

Behaviour can change throughout life through learning, experience, feedback, and environmental modification.
