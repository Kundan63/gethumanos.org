# Prevention

> *How lifestyle medicine, preventive medicine, vaccination, screening, and health promotion work together to stop disease before it starts — or catch it early enough that treatment still works.*

---

# What This Section Covers

Once you understand how disease develops, the next question is how to stop it. Prevention covers the tools medicine uses to reduce risk, protect populations, and catch problems before they become serious.
