# Health & Medicine

<p class="domain-tagline">Why humans become unhealthy, and how health can be protected or restored.</p>
</div>

> [!abstract]
> Health and Medicine is the study of how the human body maintains normal function, why diseases occur, how they are detected, prevented, treated, and how health can be preserved throughout life. Health is not simply the absence of disease — it is the dynamic ability of the body and mind to maintain balance, adapt to challenges, recover from injury, and function physically, mentally, and socially.

# Why This Actually Matters

Understanding Health & Medicine helps people prevent disease, detect illness early, make informed healthcare decisions, understand medical advice, and extend healthy lifespan. Many of today's leading diseases are preventable or can be delayed through healthy behaviors and timely medical care.
