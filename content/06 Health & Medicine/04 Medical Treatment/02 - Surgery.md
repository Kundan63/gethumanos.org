# Surgery

> *Surgery is a branch of medicine that uses operative procedures to diagnose, treat, repair, remove, or replace diseased, injured, or abnormal tissues and organs. Modern surgery combines anatomy, physiology, pathology, anesthesia, sterile technique, and technology to restore health, relieve suffering, prevent disability, or save lives. It ranges from minor outpatient procedures to highly complex organ transplantation and robotic surgery. Safe surgery is considered an essential component of universal healthcare because millions of lives depend on timely access to surgical and anesthesia services.* :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

# What is Surgery?

Surgery is a medical treatment in which trained surgeons perform procedures on the body to:

- Diagnose disease
- Remove diseased tissue
- Repair damaged structures
- Restore function
- Prevent complications
- Save life

Most surgical procedures involve:

- Incisions
- Specialized instruments
- Sterile techniques
- Anesthesia
- Careful postoperative recovery

Surgery is also called an **operation**. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Why does Surgery exist?

Many diseases cannot be cured by medicines alone.

Examples include:

- Broken bones
- Appendicitis
- Blocked arteries
- Tumors
- Internal bleeding
- Organ failure
- Birth defects

Surgery physically changes the body to restore normal structure or function when other treatments are insufficient. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

# The Big Question

> **When is cutting the body the safest and most effective way to heal it?**

Surgery is used when the expected benefit of physically repairing, removing, or replacing tissue outweighs the risks of an operation.


---

## 🔍 Diagnostic Surgery

Performed to determine the cause of disease.

Examples:

- Biopsy
- Exploratory surgery

---

## ❤️ Curative Surgery

Removes the source of disease.

Examples:

- Appendectomy
- Tumor removal
- Gallbladder removal

---

## 🔧 Reconstructive Surgery

Restores normal appearance or function.

Examples:

- Fracture repair
- Burn reconstruction
- Ligament repair

---

## 🌿 Palliative Surgery

Reduces symptoms without curing disease.

Examples:

- Relieving bowel obstruction
- Reducing tumor pressure

---

## 🫀 Transplant Surgery

Replaces a damaged organ.

Examples:

- Kidney transplant
- Liver transplant
- Heart transplant

---

## 🛡 Preventive Surgery

Performed to reduce future disease risk.

Examples:

- Removal of precancerous polyps
- Risk-reducing mastectomy in selected high-risk individuals

---

## 🎨 Cosmetic Surgery

Improves appearance.

May be:

- Cosmetic
- Reconstructive after trauma or disease

# Open vs Minimally Invasive Surgery

## Open Surgery

- Larger incision
- Greater tissue exposure
- Longer recovery

---

## Minimally Invasive Surgery

Examples:

- Laparoscopy
- Endoscopy
- Arthroscopy
- Robotic surgery

Advantages may include:

- Smaller incisions
- Less pain
- Lower infection risk
- Faster recovery
- Shorter hospital stay

## Preoperative Care

Includes:

- Medical evaluation
- Blood tests
- Imaging
- Consent
- Medication review
- Fasting
- Surgical planning

---

## Intraoperative Care

During surgery:

- Sterile technique
- Monitoring
- Anesthesia
- Surgical procedure
- Bleeding control

---

## Postoperative Care

Focuses on:

- Pain control
- Infection prevention
- Wound care
- Early mobilization
- Rehabilitation
- Follow-up

---

# Anesthesia

Surgery often requires anesthesia.

Types include:

### Local

Only a small area becomes numb.

### Regional

Blocks sensation in a larger body region.

Examples:

- Epidural
- Spinal anesthesia

### General

Produces unconsciousness and prevents pain during major surgery. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

---

# Common Surgical Procedures

Examples include:

- Appendectomy
- Cesarean section
- Hernia repair
- Joint replacement
- Cataract surgery
- Coronary artery bypass graft (CABG)
- Hip fracture repair
- Cholecystectomy
- Organ transplantation

---

# Risks of Surgery

Possible complications include:

- Bleeding
- Infection
- Blood clots
- Damage to nearby organs
- Anesthesia complications
- Poor wound healing
- Scar formation

Modern surgical practice aims to minimize these risks through standardized safety protocols. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

---

# Safe Surgery

The WHO **Safe Surgery Saves Lives** initiative emphasizes:

- Correct patient
- Correct procedure
- Correct surgical site
- Safe anesthesia
- Infection prevention
- Effective teamwork
- Surgical checklists

These measures significantly reduce preventable complications. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

---

# Recovery

Recovery depends on:

- Type of surgery
- Age
- Overall health
- Nutrition
- Rehabilitation
- Complications

Recovery may take:

- Hours
- Days
- Weeks
- Months

---

# Nutrition After Surgery

Recovery is supported by:

### Protein

Supports tissue repair.

### Vitamins

Especially:

- Vitamin C
- Vitamin A

### Minerals

- Zinc
- Iron (when needed)

### Hydration

Supports circulation and healing.

A balanced diet is generally preferred over supplements unless deficiencies exist.

---

# Lifestyle & Rehabilitation

Evidence supports:

- Early mobilization (when medically appropriate)
- Smoking cessation
- Good nutrition
- Physical therapy
- Adequate sleep
- Blood sugar control

These measures improve healing and reduce complications.

---

# Evidence-Based Natural Support

| Intervention | Possible Benefit | Evidence |
|--------------|------------------|----------|
| High-protein diet | Tissue repair | ★★★★★ |
| Adequate hydration | Recovery | ★★★★★ |
| Exercise rehabilitation | Functional recovery | ★★★★★ |
| Smoking cessation | Wound healing | ★★★★★ |
| Vitamin replacement (if deficient) | Healing support | ★★★★★ |

No herbal remedy replaces surgery when surgery is medically indicated.

---

# Ayurveda & Traditional Medicine

Traditional supportive practices may include:

- Turmeric
- Amla
- Ashwagandha
- Yoga (during rehabilitation)
- Breathing exercises

### Current Scientific Evidence

Some traditional practices may improve comfort, mobility, or recovery during rehabilitation. However, they **cannot replace necessary surgical procedures** and should only be used alongside evidence-based postoperative care.

### Safety

- Inform surgeons about all herbal supplements before surgery because some increase bleeding risk or interact with anesthesia.
- Follow postoperative wound-care instructions carefully.
- Fever, severe pain, wound discharge, or heavy bleeding require urgent medical evaluation.

---

# Prevention

Many surgeries can be prevented by reducing disease risk.

Examples:

- Healthy diet
- Exercise
- Vaccination
- Blood pressure control
- Diabetes management
- Injury prevention
- Cancer screening

Not all surgery is preventable, but many operations result from diseases influenced by lifestyle.

# Why should humans care?

Surgery has transformed human survival.

It allows doctors to:

- Save lives
- Restore movement
- Replace organs
- Repair injuries
- Treat cancers
- Correct birth defects

Access to safe surgery is considered a fundamental component of modern healthcare. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

## Philosophy

> **Surgery is the art and science of restoring the body by physically correcting what disease, injury, or congenital conditions have disrupted.**

# Applying this Knowledge

- Understand why surgery is recommended before consenting.
- Follow preoperative instructions carefully.
- Disclose all medicines and supplements.
- Participate in rehabilitation after surgery.
- Seek urgent care for signs of postoperative complications.

# HumanOS Principle

Surgery is the branch of medicine that physically repairs, removes, reconstructs, or replaces diseased or injured tissues when other treatments are insufficient. Modern surgery depends on accurate diagnosis, safe anesthesia, sterile technique, skilled teamwork, and postoperative rehabilitation. Together with pharmacology and preventive medicine, surgery forms one of the fundamental pillars of evidence-based healthcare. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

---

# Key Insight

> **Surgery is the deliberate modification of the human body to restore health. Understanding when surgery is needed, how it is performed, its risks, benefits, and recovery process shows that successful surgical care depends not only on the operation itself but also on preparation, safety, rehabilitation, and long-term prevention.** :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}
