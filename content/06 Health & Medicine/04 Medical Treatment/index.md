# Medical Treatment

> *How pharmacology, surgery, rehabilitation, critical care, emergency medicine, and palliative care restore function, relieve suffering, and save lives once disease or injury takes hold.*

---

# What This Section Covers

Once disease has developed, medicine intervenes. This section covers the major branches of treatment — from drugs and operations to intensive care, emergency response, and care focused on comfort rather than cure.
