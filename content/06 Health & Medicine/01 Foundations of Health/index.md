# Foundations of Health

> *What health actually is, how your body maintains balance through homeostasis, why disease develops when that balance breaks down, and how doctors work out what has gone wrong.*

---

# What This Section Covers

Foundations of Health lays the groundwork for everything else in Health & Medicine. Before disease can be prevented or treated, you first need to understand what health is, how the body defends its own stability, why things go wrong, and how that process gets diagnosed.
