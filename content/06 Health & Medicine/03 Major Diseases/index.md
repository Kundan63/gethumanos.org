# Major Diseases

> *How the world's leading causes of illness and death develop — from infectious diseases and cancer to cardiovascular, metabolic, respiratory, and neurological conditions.*

---

# What This Section Covers

When prevention isn't enough, disease takes hold. This section walks through the major categories of disease that account for most of the world's illness and death, organized by the body system or process they affect.

<div class="card-grid">
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/01---infectious-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129440;</span><span class="os-card-title">Infectious Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Illnesses caused by bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites, and prions.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/02---chronic-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#8987;</span><span class="os-card-title">Chronic Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Long-lasting noncommunicable conditions responsible for most deaths worldwide.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/03---cardiovascular-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#10084;&#65039;</span><span class="os-card-title">Cardiovascular Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Disorders of the heart and blood vessels, the leading cause of death worldwide.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/04---diabetes--and--metabolic-disorders" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129830;</span><span class="os-card-title">Diabetes &amp; Metabolic Disorders</span><span class="os-card-desc">Conditions where the body's regulation of glucose, fats, and hormones becomes impaired.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/05---cancer" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#127895;&#65039;</span><span class="os-card-title">Cancer</span><span class="os-card-desc">Diseases in which abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and invade surrounding tissue.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/06---respiratory-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129749;</span><span class="os-card-title">Respiratory Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Disorders affecting the airways, lungs, and gas exchange.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/07---digestive-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#127869;&#65039;</span><span class="os-card-title">Digestive Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Disorders of the gastrointestinal tract, liver, pancreas, and gallbladder.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/08---kidney-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129830;</span><span class="os-card-title">Kidney Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Disorders that impair the kidneys' ability to filter blood and regulate fluids.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/09---liver-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129514;</span><span class="os-card-title">Liver Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Disorders that damage the liver's ability to metabolize, detoxify, and process nutrients.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/10---brain--and--neurological-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129504;</span><span class="os-card-title">Brain &amp; Neurological Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Disorders of the brain, spinal cord, nerves, and muscles — now the leading cause of disability worldwide.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/11---mental-health-disorders" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129513;</span><span class="os-card-title">Mental Health Disorders</span><span class="os-card-desc">Disturbances in cognition, emotional regulation, or behavior affecting about 1 in 7 people worldwide.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/12---autoimmune-diseases" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#128737;&#65039;</span><span class="os-card-title">Autoimmune Diseases</span><span class="os-card-desc">Conditions where the immune system mistakenly attacks the body's own tissues.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/13---musculoskeletal-disorders" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129437;</span><span class="os-card-title">Musculoskeletal Disorders</span><span class="os-card-desc">Conditions affecting bones, muscles, joints, and connective tissue — the leading cause of disability globally.</span></a>
<a class="os-card" href="/06-health--and--medicine/03-major-diseases/14---genetic-disorders" data-no-popover="true"><span class="os-card-icon">&#129516;</span><span class="os-card-title">Genetic Disorders</span><span class="os-card-desc">Diseases caused wholly or partly by changes in DNA.</span></a>
