# Society & Civilization

<p class="domain-tagline">How individual humans become civilizations.</p>
</div>

> [!abstract]
> Society is the organized system of human relationships, institutions, and interactions through which people live together, cooperate, and develop shared ways of life. Civilization is the advanced stage of organized society characterized by cities, governments, laws, economies, education, technology, writing, and complex social institutions. This section explains how billions of individuals organize themselves into functioning societies, and how those societies evolve over time.

# Why This Actually Matters

Every person lives within society. Society influences health, education, opportunities, wealth, freedom, relationships, identity, and security. Understanding society helps people become informed citizens, responsible leaders, and thoughtful contributors to human progress.
